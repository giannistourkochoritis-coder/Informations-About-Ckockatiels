About Cockatiels

Cockatiels are beautiful and intelligent birds that come from Australia. They are known for their orange cheeks, yellow crests and friendly personalities. They love singing, whistling and spending time with their owners.

Different Cockatiel Colors

Lutino

Yellow and white feathers with orange cheeks.

Grey

Grey feathers with beautiful orange cheeks.

Whiteface

A beautiful mutation with a white face.

Fun Facts About Cockatiels

  • Cockatiels originally come from Australia.
  • They love singing and whistling.
  • They use their crest to express emotions.
  • They are very social birds.
  • They can learn to imitate sounds.

How to Take Care of Cockatiels

Cockatiels need a healthy and balanced diet, fresh water and a clean environment. They also need daily attention, toys, exercise and enough space to move around. Taking care of a cockatiel requires love, patience and responsibility!
